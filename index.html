NOT FINANCIAL ADVICE. These are rules-based, probabilistic signals computed automatically from public market data. They are wrong often. Markers are drawn only with data available at that bar (no look-ahead); the backtest replays each past BUY with its stop and a 2R target on the loaded candles (if the stop and target are both hit in the same candle, it counts as a loss). Funding and macro use the latest values only, so history is scored without them. Past hit-rate does not predict future results.
